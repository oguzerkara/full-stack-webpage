## Use Cases
### User Personas

**Actor / Persona 1: Immigrant Who Does Not Know German Language**

**Interests:** Family, learning new languages, cultural integration

**Use Case: Language Learning and Cultural Integration, public events**

Goals / Use case requirements

- In also English, Turkish and maybe in Arabic language,
- German language learning app like DW,
- public events,
- bus schedules,
- emergency numbers,
- information desk locations & governmental locations
- Social governmental societies or awareness web-sites

System:

The Immigrant uses the web page to find a translator, or dictionary api, or language learning app link, and maybe also community groups that helps immigrants to integrate into the local culture. 

S/he uses maps or descriptions of transportation roots, and schedules, maybe also DB link or API to check the schedules. 

S/he checks the events from the time schedule of city hall. And can reach the information of information desks and governmental addresses, phone numbers, locations and working times. 

S/he also looks for information on legal matters and services that can assist her/him in his native language. 

S/he uses the web-site to reach emergency numbers quickly.
